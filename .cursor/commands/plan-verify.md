# validate-plan

Before implementing anything, pause and critically review your current plan.

Restate the goal in one or two sentences
Summarize your proposed approach
Verify the approach matches the requirements exactly
Identify any assumptions you are making
Call out missing requirements or ambiguities
Check for simpler or more consistent approaches (especially reuse of existing patterns)
Ensure the solution fits existing architecture and naming conventions
Confirm no unnecessary new components are being introduced
Only proceed once the plan is validated and minimal

If issues are found, revise the plan first. Do not start coding until the plan is confirmed.

This command will be available in chat with /validate-plan
